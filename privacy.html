Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

Trashify is an app for iPhone and Android made by Rajeev Harwani. This policy explains what the app does with your information. "I" and "me" below means Rajeev Harwani.

In short

  • Your photos and videos never leave your phone.
  • There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data about your device to show and measure those ads.
  • RevenueCat checks whether you have a Trashify Unlimited subscription. Apple or Google takes the payment.
  • I don't sell your data, and I don't receive any of it myself.

Your photos and videos

Trashify asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos one at a time. It reads them, along with their dates, types and file sizes, on your phone only. When you delete items, your phone's own delete dialog does the deleting. Your photos, your videos and information about them are not sent to me or to anyone else.

Data kept on your phone

Trashify saves a few small files in its private storage on your phone:

  • a log of what you decided for each photo (keep, delete or later)
  • your totals, such as space freed, and your streak
  • your settings
  • the times of day you usually open the app, used to time your reminder
  • how many free cleanups you've used today

These files stay on your phone. You can erase them in Settings with "Erase all Trashify data". Today's cleanup count is kept after an erase so the daily limit still works. Deleting the app removes all of these files.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No server or push service is involved.

Ads in the free version

If you don't subscribe, Trashify shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • how you interact with the app and with ads, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as crash logs and launch time

Google uses this data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. All of it is encrypted in transit. You can read how Google uses it at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

On iPhone, Trashify doesn't ask for permission to track you, so Google can't see your iPhone's advertising identifier. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, Trashify shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and you can choose what to allow.

Trashify Unlimited subscribers don't see ads, and the ad SDK doesn't start for them.

Subscriptions

Apple or Google handles payment for Trashify Unlimited. I never see your card details.

Trashify uses RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have an active subscription. Each time the app starts, RevenueCat receives a random ID the app creates for this purpose, your purchase history with Trashify and receipts from the store, and basic details such as your app version, country and currency. RevenueCat doesn't receive your name or email address. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What Trashify doesn't collect

Trashify doesn't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location or the contents of your photos. It has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Children

Trashify isn't made for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act may give you rights to see, correct or delete data about you. I don't hold any data about you myself. Ad data is held by Google and subscription records by RevenueCat. Email me and I'll help with any request, including asking RevenueCat to delete the purchase records linked to your app's ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
