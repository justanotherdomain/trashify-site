Questions, bugs or ideas? Email rajeevh14@gmail.com. Please tell me your phone model and the app version, which is at the bottom of Settings.

Where do deleted photos go?

On iPhone they go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can get them back for 30 days. On Android 11 and later, deleting is permanent.

Why does Trashify need access to all my photos?

To show you every photo and video, month by month, and delete the ones you mark. Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. If you allowed only some photos, you can add more in Settings → Choose more photos.

How do I cancel Trashify Unlimited?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Trashify. On Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Trashify. You keep Unlimited until the end of the month you paid for.

I bought Unlimited but it's not showing

Open the Unlimited screen in Trashify and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

How does the free daily limit work?

You can clean 500 photos and videos a day for free, with a short ad every 50. If you run out, you can watch a longer ad for 100 more. The count starts again at midnight.
